Meet Our Team

Who we are

SkyBrella is an innovative technology company that combines drone engineering with everyday convenience. Our intelligent drone umbrellas fly above users automatically, providing protection from rain and sunshine without needing to be held.

Our story

Founded in 2026, SkyBrella began with a simple idea: "What if an umbrella could follow you?" Since then, our team has developed advanced drone systems capable of tracking users safely and accurately while providing hands-free shelter.
