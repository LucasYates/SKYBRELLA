Welcome to SkyBrella umbrellas were we provide hands-free protection from rain while you go about your day. Whether you're walking to work, shopping, or enjoying outdoor activities, our smart flying umbrellas offer convenience, comfort, and a glimpse into the future of personal weather protection.

information

Smart Umbrella is a modern umbrella designed to make everyday life easier. With built-in GPS tracking, users can quickly locate their umbrella through the mobile app if it is lost or left behind. The umbrella also uses Bluetooth technology to alert users when they move too far away from it. In addition, the Smart Umbrella can sync with weather services to provide rain alerts, ensuring users are always prepared before heading outside.

SkyBrella Drone Umbrella, a revolutionary way to stay dry without holding an umbrella. Using smart follow-me technology, the drone hovers safely above your head, providing continuous protection from rain while keeping your hands free.
